## MANIC!! / Kaai Yuki

<LinkCard href="https://www.nicovideo.jp/watch/sm46700317" />

The soft and gentle vibe is cute.

It goes really well with Yuki.

The lyrics are great too, having a sense of vitality while still feeling fleeting.

I like the rolling sound playing in the background.

The sound of raindrops popping up at key points creates a nice atmosphere for the song too....

It makes me want to get wild and make some noise too!!

## Summer Project / Hatsune Miku

<LinkCard href="https://www.nicovideo.jp/watch/sm46703589" />

The cicadas at the beginning are already so good.

A live-action MV, nice.

This atmosphere brings back memories of back in the day.

Those days that everyone used to have.

Ah, the chorus is great. I love it!

It really stimulates the inner child sleeping inside me. I appreciate that.

It makes me want to make something.

Maybe a milk carton sofa would be nice.

The feeling of "it's coming" right before the final chorus is way too good.

Thank you for the great song.

## The Conversation Continues / higma feat. AI Nurse Robo_Type T

<LinkCard href="https://www.nicovideo.jp/watch/sm46688273" />

It is a somber song.

Wait, it is a song that gets me grooving disguised as a somber song.

You tricked me!

It is a live-action MV. Nice.

This is my first time hearing this voicebank too, but it is a cute, delicate voice.

The heavy bass in the chorus really hits the spot.

Let's talk about the lyrics.

I feel like it is saying, why don't we just take it easy and go through these casual days, these mundane, saturated days?

Man, the heavy bass in the chorus is just too good.

The conversation continues, and as long as you and I are alive, the days will keep going on.

That is why we should keep walking forward.

That is how I interpreted it.

It is probably a distorted interpretation, though.
