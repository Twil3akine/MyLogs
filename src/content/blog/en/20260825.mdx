It is me.

The Vocaloid Collection 2026 Summer has come to an end.

I do not usually listen to things like The Vocaloid Collection at all, but a few songs that piqued my interest showed up on my Twitter timeline, so I thought I would give various tracks a listen.

So, here are a few songs I listened to during the event that made me think, "I am really glad I listened to this."

This is not a ranking. In no particular order.

## 初音ミク『恋』(Hatsune Miku "Love")

<LinkCard href="https://www.nicovideo.jp/watch/sm46620250" />

A proud 2nd place.

I think there was a very real world line where this took the championship. It is such a complete song.

I learned about this artist through the song "個々々々々々人" ("Kokokokokokojin"), and they are truly skilled at creating a world view.

Before you know it, you are drawn in, captivated, and then sent back. They make that kind of song.

This track also seems structured to be easy to recommend to others, with features like:

- The catchy phrase "Ah-ah"
- Lyrics that are not too difficult
- An MV featuring a good old-fashioned single illustration
