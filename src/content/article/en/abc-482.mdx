### Problem C [Band Count](https://atcoder.jp/contests/abc482/tasks/abc482_c)

When I first saw it, it looked super tedious, but it turned out all I needed to do was store rows and columns separately, sort them, and perform binary search. For some reason, I had a mental breakdown thinking it would be better to use coordinate compression.

Generalizing `u`, `d`, `l`, `r`, and letting $x, y \; (x < y)$, the answer is:

$$
C_{1 \rightarrow y} - C_{1 \rightarrow (x - 1)}
$$

I wasted time on a library I had never used before, which was a real shame...

Don't do things you aren't used to. Stay calm.
