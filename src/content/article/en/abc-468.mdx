It is me.

I participated in [ABC468](https://atcoder.jp/contests/abc468). I got 4 problems solved with 3 penalties and a very slow time. It has been a while since I lost this badly. I cannot forgive the writer of this contest until they return my rating.

However, since problem `C` was educational, I will consider it barely okay.

---

### Problem A [Maximal Value](https://atcoder.jp/contests/abc468/tasks/abc468_a)

I misread the problem and was late to realize that it was about finding the number of local maxima.

---

### Problem B [Corridor Watch](https://atcoder.jp/contests/abc468/tasks/abc468_b)

I misread this one too, somehow thinking that $D = 1$. I got off to a late start.

The approach is to fill the grid within distance $D$ before and after `G` and then count the number of empty cells.

---

### Problem C [Between P and Q](https://atcoder.jp/contests/abc468/tasks/abc468_c)

Now, for the problematic Problem C.

I figured out within two seconds that it was an exhaustive search of permutations, but I spent way too much time actually solving it from there...

At first, I was struggling with conditions based on the leading digit, but even after getting two penalties, I couldn't see a way to solve it. When I stopped to rethink, I realized I could solve it by converting the permutations into numerical values.

Something like $[1, 3, 2] \to 132$.

Furthermore, I found out after the contest that you can compare tuples in Rust, so what were my two penalties even for...

So, if I had known that, the problem would have been reduced to simply finding the number of permutations $A$ such that $P \lt A \lt Q$.

---

### Problem D [Pre-Palindrome](https://atcoder.jp/contests/abc468/tasks/abc468_d)

Since $|S| \le 10^4$, it was obvious that $O(|S|^2)$ would pass. I thought I would finish it instantly with an exhaustive search, but the code I wrote ended up being $O(|S|^3)$. This is exactly the kind of mistake I always make...

I wondered why I was getting a <Marker type="warning">TLE</Marker>, and when I looked at the code, it was clearly $O(|S|^3)$. Everyone, please be careful about computational complexity.

From there, I thought I could solve it by doing something like Manacher's algorithm, so I tried tinkering with a Manacher library, but it didn't go well, so I wrote it from scratch.

---

### Problem E [Sum of Average](https://atcoder.jp/contests/abc468/tasks/abc468_e)

I can't verbalize it well, but I have an intuition that problems involving double sums work out better if you look at them "vertically" rather than "horizontally." I wanted to turn that into something concrete, but I simply couldn't solve it.

I usually fail to solve problems involving formula manipulation, so I feel like I'm in a pretty bad spot...
