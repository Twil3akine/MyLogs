the birthday of my idol, <h1><Marker>Hatsune Miku</Marker></h1>

Continuing from last year, I want to retweet the massive amount of Miku illustrations that get posted, right? But that would totally clutter people's timelines, right?

So, last year I recommended muted RTs, but then I thought, <Marker type="danger">"Why do I have to be the one treading lightly?"</Marker>

So, I thought it would be great to just make an account where I do not have to worry about anything. So I made one.

I haven't decided on any specific management policy for this account, but since I am using the internet anyway, I don't want to care about what my followers think. I hope to post things like that, as well as stuff like illustrations that feel a bit embarrassing to share with the general public.

Right now, of course, it is flooded with retweets of Miku illustrations.
