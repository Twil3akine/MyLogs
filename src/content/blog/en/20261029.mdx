## What was created

I thought it would be nice if the OGP content changed for each article anyway, so I had it make dynamic OGPs for me.

Then, using Figma's MCP, I had Muse create a draft, made fine adjustments myself, and fed them back into Muse. That was the general workflow.

<Spoiler title="What prompts were used (from Muse's logs)" client:visible>

Here are the prompts in the actual order I gave them, to share in this article:

- Can we dynamically change the OGP images for blogs and articles based on the title?
- (Looking at the bottom of the first draft) Ugly, unbelievable
- The title should be centered vertically and horizontally. Make the top tag and date larger, and move them down
- (Looking at the bottom of the second draft) Truly unbelievable
- For the bottom, use a colored bar like GitHub's language gauge. Use brand colors like Nix or Go
- Align the description with the left edge of the title
- Wait, maybe a chibi character sitting on top of the whole thing would be better
- Something like your current icon
- Why don't we build it once using Figma MCP?
- Make the Article tag orange
- Let's make it lying down on its stomach
- Proceed to implement it in MyLogs with this design
</Spoiler>
